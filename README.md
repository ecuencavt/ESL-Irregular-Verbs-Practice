# ESL Irregular Verbs Practice
## For 4th ESO Students in Catalonia

Welcome! This interactive tool helps you master English irregular verbs through engaging activities and quizzes.

---

## 📚 Contents

1. **[Verb List](./01-VERB-LIST.md)** - Complete irregular verbs with translations to Catalan
2. **[Practice Exercises](./02-EXERCISES.md)** - Fill-in-the-blank, matching, and translation activities
3. **[Interactive Quizzes](./03-QUIZZES.md)** - Self-check quizzes with answer keys
4. **[Games & Activities](./04-GAMES.md)** - Fun learning games

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will:
- ✅ Recognize and use 50+ common irregular verbs
- ✅ Know the base, past, and past participle forms
- ✅ Use irregular verbs correctly in sentences
- ✅ Distinguish between regular and irregular verbs

---

## 💡 How to Use This Resource

1. Start with the **Verb List** - Learn the forms
2. Do the **Practice Exercises** - Apply your knowledge
3. Try the **Interactive Quizzes** - Check your progress
4. Play the **Games** - Have fun while learning
5. Take the **Tests** - Assess your mastery

---

## 🇨🇦 Catalan Translations Included

All verbs include Catalan translations to help you understand better!

**Example:**
- **go** (anar) - went (vaig anar) - gone (anat/ada)

---

## 📊 Difficulty Levels

- **Level 1 (Easy)**: Most common verbs (be, have, go, do)
- **Level 2 (Medium)**: Common irregular verbs (eat, drink, come, make)
- **Level 3 (Hard)**: Advanced irregular verbs (arise, bid, breed, catch)

---

## 📝 Tips for Success

✨ **Study Tip 1**: Group verbs by pattern (similar endings or sound changes)
✨ **Study Tip 2**: Say the verbs aloud - hear them and say them!
✨ **Study Tip 3**: Use flashcards - test yourself repeatedly
✨ **Study Tip 4**: Write example sentences with each verb
✨ **Study Tip 5**: Practice a little every day, not all at once!
