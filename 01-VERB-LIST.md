## 📝 Notes:
- Some verbs have **two possible past tense forms** (e.g., burned/burnt, learned/learnt)
- The **past participle** is used with "have" or "has" (She **has** gone)
- Pay attention to **vowel changes** (sing→sang→sung, eat→ate→eaten)
- **Practice these daily** - repetition is key!
